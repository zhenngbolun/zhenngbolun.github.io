Openings

I am looking for self-motivated M.S. and Ph.D. students with a solid background in programming and mathematics, working on computer vision and computational photography. Students with a genuine interest in low-level vision — restoration, imaging pipelines, HDR, or efficient inference — are especially encouraged to reach out. Please send me an email with your CV, transcripts, and a short note about what you would like to work on. English or Chinese, either is fine.

Research

Computational Photography & HDR

HDR video reconstruction from limited or alternating exposures, dual-camera acquisition for temporally stable HDR, single-image HDR rendering, Quad-Bayer demosaicing, and frequency-domain image demoiréing.

Image & Video Restoration

Low-light enhancement, compression artifact reduction for JPEG and HEVC, super-resolution and deblurring, with attention to how these models behave under real-world noise and on resource-constrained hardware.

Dense Prediction & Perception

Salient object detection across RGB-D, video, remote sensing and adverse-weather settings; infrared small target detection; UAV-view geo-localization and cross-view matching.

Reliable & Multimodal Vision

Out-of-distribution detection, adversarial transferability, vision–language alignment for multimodal large models, and perceptual quality assessment of restored content.
